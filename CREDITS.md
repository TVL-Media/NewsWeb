## Sample data images

The optional sample data (installer → "Install sample data") uses **72 photos shipped in
`database/seeders/demo-photos/`**. Every one is in the **public domain** or released under **CC0**,
so you may use, change and sell them without asking or crediting anyone. The authors are still listed
below as a courtesy, and each sample story shows its photographer as the photo credit.

They were found with [Openverse](https://openverse.org) and checked by hand. Photos showing well-known
people, children, brand logos or posters were left out. Open source websites may themselves label
photos wrongly, so if you plan to keep any sample photo on a live site, check its source page first.
The sample stories are fictional, and the photos only illustrate their topic.

Want your own photos in the demo? Put JPG files in `storage/app/demo/` with the same names
(for example `cricket-1.jpg`). They are used before the shipped ones. If neither exists, a plain
image is drawn on your server instead.
